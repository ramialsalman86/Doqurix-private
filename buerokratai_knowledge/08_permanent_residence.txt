RESIDENCE PERMIT EXTENSION AND PERMANENT RESIDENCE
===================================================
Source: BAMF, Ausländerbehörde, Aufenthaltsgesetz
Last Updated: January 2026

RESIDENCE PERMIT EXTENSION (VERLÄNGERUNG)
-----------------------------------------

When to apply:
- Apply BEFORE your current permit expires
- Recommended: 2-3 months before expiration
- At minimum: Before expiry date
- Late applications may cause problems (gaps in legality)

Where to apply:
- Ausländerbehörde (immigration office) of your city
- Book appointment in advance (often weeks of wait time)
- Same office that issued your current permit

Fiktionsbescheinigung (Fiction Certificate):
- If permit expires during processing
- Proves your status is "continued" while waiting
- Allows you to continue working/staying legally
- Applied for at Ausländerbehörde
- Fee: Usually free

GENERAL REQUIREMENTS FOR EXTENSION
----------------------------------
Most residence permit extensions require:

1. Valid passport (at least 6 months validity recommended)
2. Biometric photos (35x45mm, current)
3. Current Meldebescheinigung (address registration, recent)
4. Proof of ongoing purpose:
   - Employment: Current work contract
   - Study: Enrollment certificate (Immatrikulationsbescheinigung)
   - Family: Proof of relationship continues
5. Health insurance confirmation
6. Proof of sufficient income/funds
7. Current residence permit (to be extended)
8. Completed application form

Fees:
- Extension: €93
- Fee may be reduced or waived in some cases

SPECIFIC EXTENSION REQUIREMENTS
-------------------------------

EU Blue Card Extension:
- Same job or new qualifying job
- Salary still above threshold
- Degree and recognition still valid
- Health insurance
- If job changed: May need new approval

Work Permit Extension:
- Continued employment
- Employer confirmation
- Federal Employment Agency approval (if originally required)

Student Visa Extension:
- Proof of enrollment
- Study progress (Leistungsnachweis)
- Proof of financing (blocked account or other)
- Health insurance

Family Visa Extension:
- Marriage/relationship continues
- Sponsor still has valid residence
- Living together
- Adequate living space and income

PERMANENT RESIDENCE (NIEDERLASSUNGSERLAUBNIS)
---------------------------------------------

What is it?
- Unlimited residence permit
- No restrictions on employment
- Not tied to specific purpose
- Can only be revoked in exceptional cases

Standard requirements (§ 9 AufenthG):
1. 5 years of legal residence with residence permit
2. Secure livelihood (no social welfare dependency)
3. 60 months pension contributions (or equivalent)
4. Adequate German language skills (B1 level)
5. Basic knowledge of German legal/social system
6. Adequate living space
7. No criminal convictions
8. Valid passport
9. Health insurance

FAST TRACK PERMANENT RESIDENCE
------------------------------

Blue Card Holders (§ 18c Abs. 2 AufenthG):
After 33 months with Blue Card:
- German language level A1
- 33 months pension contributions

After 21 months with Blue Card:
- German language level B1
- 21 months pension contributions

Highly Qualified (§ 18c Abs. 1 AufenthG):
- Scientists with special knowledge
- Teachers/researchers with special functions
- Specialists with exceptional salary
- Can get immediate permanent residence

Self-employed (§ 21 Abs. 4 AufenthG):
- After 3 years with successful business
- Business meets expectations
- Livelihood secured

INTEGRATION COURSE COMPLETION
-----------------------------
For most permanent residence applications:
- Integration course completion OR
- B1 language certificate AND
- Knowledge of German society ("Leben in Deutschland" test)

Integration course includes:
- 600 hours German language
- 100 hours orientation course (German law, history, culture)
- Final tests: DTZ (German Test for Immigrants) + LiD

Test "Leben in Deutschland":
- 33 questions from catalog of 300+
- 17 correct = passed
- Tests knowledge of German society
- Available in many cities

ABSCHIEBUNGSSCHUTZ (DEPORTATION PROTECTION)
-------------------------------------------
With Niederlassungserlaubnis:
- Very difficult to deport
- Only possible for:
  * Serious crimes
  * Threat to public security
  * Fraud in obtaining permit
- Family ties and residence length considered

ERLAUBNIS ZUM DAUERAUFENTHALT-EU (EU LONG-TERM RESIDENCE)
---------------------------------------------------------
Alternative to Niederlassungserlaubnis

Requirements similar to Niederlassungserlaubnis plus:
- 5 years continuous residence in Germany
- Main residence must be Germany

Benefits over Niederlassungserlaubnis:
- Right to live in other EU countries (except Denmark, Ireland)
- Can work in other EU countries (conditions apply)
- Easier family reunification in other EU countries

CITIZENSHIP VS. PERMANENT RESIDENCE
-----------------------------------
Niederlassungserlaubnis:
- Still foreign citizen
- Still need passport
- Still registered as foreigner
- Can vote in local EU elections only
- Can lose status if live abroad too long

German Citizenship (Einbürgerung):
- Become German citizen
- German passport
- Full voting rights
- Cannot be deported
- Usually requires giving up other citizenship (exceptions)

Requirements for citizenship:
- 8 years residence (reduced to 6 or 5 with integration)
- Niederlassungserlaubnis or equivalent
- German language B1
- Pass citizenship test (33 questions)
- Renounce previous citizenship (with exceptions)
- No criminal record
- Can support yourself

AUSLÄNDERBEHÖRDE TIPS
---------------------
1. Book appointment early (weeks in advance)
2. Bring ALL documents (copies AND originals)
3. Documents should be recent (within 3 months)
4. Bring extra passport photos
5. Arrive on time (early if possible)
6. Be patient (long wait times common)
7. Bring German speaker if needed (or hire interpreter)
8. Keep copies of everything you submit
9. Ask for receipt of application
10. Note name of officer handling your case

COMMON PROBLEMS
---------------
Problem: Appointment not available before permit expires
Solution: Apply for Fiktionsbescheinigung

Problem: Missing documents
Solution: Get list in advance, prepare everything

Problem: Income too low
Solution: May need to show savings, get declaration of support

Problem: German language not sufficient
Solution: Take integration course, get certified

Problem: Gaps in employment
Solution: Explain gaps, show current employment
