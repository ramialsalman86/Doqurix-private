INTEGRATION COURSE (INTEGRATIONSKURS)
=====================================
Source: BAMF (Federal Office for Migration and Refugees)
Last Updated: January 2026

OVERVIEW
--------
Integration course is Germany's official program for immigrants.
Combines German language course with civic orientation.
May be mandatory depending on residence status.

WHO MUST ATTEND (MANDATORY)
---------------------------
Integration course is MANDATORY for:

1. New immigrants who cannot communicate in German
2. Recipients of certain social benefits (Bürgergeld)
3. Those required by residence permit conditions
4. People ordered by Ausländerbehörde

Exemption from mandatory attendance if:
- Already have B1 German level (with certificate)
- Attending comparable education
- Cannot attend due to health/family reasons
- Age/circumstances make it unreasonable

WHO CAN ATTEND (VOLUNTARY)
--------------------------
Can apply voluntarily:
- EU citizens
- German citizens with migration background
- Migrants with secure residence status
- People whose mandatory period has expired

Late resettlers (Spätaussiedler) and their families:
- Special entitlement
- Free of charge

COURSE STRUCTURE
----------------

1. LANGUAGE COURSE (Sprachkurs)
   - 600 lesson units (45 minutes each)
   - 6 modules, 100 units each
   - Target: B1 German level
   
   Content:
   - Everyday communication
   - Work-related language
   - Healthcare, education, shopping
   - Letters, forms, appointments

2. ORIENTATION COURSE (Orientierungskurs)
   - 100 lesson units
   - German legal system
   - German history
   - German culture and values
   - Rights and responsibilities

TOTAL: 700 lesson units (regular course)

SPECIAL INTEGRATION COURSES
---------------------------

1. Intensive Course (Intensivkurs)
   - 430 units total
   - For fast learners
   - Same content, faster pace

2. Course with Literacy (Alphabetisierungskurs)
   - Up to 1,200 units
   - For people learning to read/write
   - More time for basics

3. Youth Integration Course (Jugendintegrationskurs)
   - Up to 1,000 units
   - For ages 16-26
   - Focus on education system
   - Career orientation

4. Parents' Course (Elternintegrationskurs)
   - 1,000 units
   - Focus on child education
   - German school system
   - Childcare options

5. Women's Course (Frauenintegrationskurs)
   - 1,000 units
   - Women-specific topics
   - Childcare often provided

COSTS
-----

For mandatory participants:
- €2.29 per lesson unit (subsidized)
- Total: ~€1,603 for 700 units
- Paid in installments per module

For Bürgergeld recipients:
- FREE (fully funded by BAMF)

For voluntary participants (EU citizens etc.):
- May have to pay full cost
- Can apply for cost reduction

Cost exemption possible if:
- Receiving Bürgergeld/social benefits
- Financial hardship proven

REFUND (50% BACK!)
------------------
Get 50% of your costs back if:
- Pass B1 exam (DTZ) within 2 years of starting
- Don't repeat any module

How to apply:
- Use BAMF refund form after passing
- Submit to BAMF or course provider
- Include exam certificate

EXAM: DTZ (Deutsch-Test für Zuwanderer)
---------------------------------------
Final exam at end of language course

Levels tested:
- A2 (basic)
- B1 (intermediate)

Exam parts:
1. Listening (25 minutes)
2. Reading (45 minutes)
3. Writing (30 minutes)
4. Speaking (15 minutes, paired)

Results:
- B1 in all parts: "B1 passed"
- B1 in some parts: Partial certificate
- Below B1: Can retake, may get free repeat course

Passing B1 is important for:
- Permanent residence applications
- Naturalization/citizenship
- Certain visa extensions

EXAM: LEBEN IN DEUTSCHLAND (LID)
--------------------------------
Test at end of orientation course

Format:
- 33 multiple choice questions
- 17 correct answers needed to pass
- 60 minutes

Topics:
- Democracy and rule of law
- German history
- Rights and duties
- Regional questions (your state)

This certificate needed for:
- Naturalization (citizenship)
- Some permanent residence paths

FINDING A COURSE
----------------

1. Get authorization certificate (Berechtigungsschein)
   - From Ausländerbehörde (for mandatory)
   - Or BAMF regional office (for voluntary)

2. Find a provider:
   - BAMF course search: bamf.de/integrationskurse
   - Search by postal code
   - Filter by course type

3. Register with provider
   - Bring authorization certificate
   - Bring ID/residence permit
   - Take placement test (Einstufungstest)

Course providers include:
- Volkshochschule (VHS) - adult education centers
- Private language schools
- Church organizations (Caritas, Diakonie)
- Berlitz, Goethe-Institut, etc.

ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENTS
-----------------------
- Regular attendance mandatory
- Absences must be excused
- Too many absences: May lose funding
- Report absences to provider promptly

Valid excuses:
- Illness (with doctor's note)
- Urgent appointments (Ausländerbehörde, etc.)
- Family emergencies

CERTIFICATE (INTEGRATION CERTIFICATE)
-------------------------------------
Receive "Zertifikat Integrationskurs" if you:
- Pass DTZ at B1 level
- Pass Leben in Deutschland test

This certificate proves:
- German language skills
- Civic knowledge
- Completion of integration requirements

Important for later applications!

B1 CERTIFICATE ALTERNATIVES
---------------------------
If you already have B1 German:
- Goethe-Zertifikat B1
- telc Deutsch B1
- ÖSD Zertifikat B1
- TestDaF (TDN 3 or higher)
- DSH (level 1 or higher)

These can exempt you from language course.

REPEATING THE COURSE
--------------------
If you don't pass:
- Can repeat 300 units free (language course)
- Must apply within 2 years of first exam
- Second exam also free
- Third attempt: Self-pay

TIMELINE EXAMPLE
----------------
Typical schedule:
- 4-5 lessons per day
- 4-5 days per week
- Full-time: ~6-7 months
- Part-time: ~12-14 months

Course types:
- Full-time (Vollzeit): Morning or afternoon
- Part-time (Teilzeit): Evenings
- Weekend courses available

ONLINE/HYBRID OPTIONS
---------------------
Since 2020, some courses offer:
- Hybrid (mix of online and in-person)
- Virtual classroom modules
- Check with provider

RIGHTS DURING COURSE
--------------------
- Childcare support (ask provider)
- Travel cost reimbursement (in some cases)
- Reduced fees if receiving benefits
- Course materials usually included

AFTER THE INTEGRATION COURSE
----------------------------
Further options:
- B2 Job-related German (berufsbezogene Sprachförderung)
- Also funded by BAMF
- Specialized courses (nursing, technical, etc.)
- Pathway to C1 level
