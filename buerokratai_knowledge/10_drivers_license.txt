DRIVER'S LICENSE IN GERMANY (FÜHRERSCHEIN)
==========================================
Source: German Road Traffic Regulations (StVG, FeV)
Last Updated: January 2026

OVERVIEW
--------
Germany accepts foreign licenses for limited time.
Most non-EU licenses must be converted eventually.
Rules depend on your country of origin and residence permit type.

USING YOUR FOREIGN LICENSE
--------------------------

EU/EEA Licenses:
- Valid indefinitely in Germany
- No conversion needed
- Just update address when it expires

International Driving Permit (IDP):
- Recommended for first 6 months
- Not a license itself - only valid WITH original license
- Translation supplement
- Get in home country before traveling

HOW LONG CAN YOU DRIVE WITH FOREIGN LICENSE?
--------------------------------------------

Tourists (up to 6 months):
- Can drive with valid foreign license
- Recommended to carry IDP
- No German license needed

Residents (registered in Germany):
- 6 months from date of registration (Anmeldung)
- After 6 months: MUST have German license
- Extension possible in some cases (up to 12 months)

Blue Card/Work Visa holders:
- Same 6-month rule
- Plan conversion early!

Students:
- May get extension during study period
- Check with local Fahrerlaubnisbehörde

CONVERSION REQUIREMENTS (Anlage 11 countries)
---------------------------------------------
Documents needed:
1. Valid passport
2. Residence permit
3. Anmeldung confirmation
4. Original foreign driver's license
5. Translation of license (by certified translator)
6. Biometric passport photo (35x45mm)
7. Eye test (Sehtest) - from optician (~€7)
8. Application form

Where to apply:
- Fahrerlaubnisbehörde (Driver's License Authority)
- Part of Bürgeramt in most cities
- Some cities: Separate Straßenverkehrsamt

Cost: €35-50 (administrative fee)
Processing time: 4-8 weeks

CONVERSION WITH PRACTICAL TEST ONLY
-----------------------------------
Some countries require only practical test, no theory:

Examples:
- Some US states not listed above
- Some Canadian provinces
- Other countries with partial agreements

Process:
1. Register at Fahrschule (driving school)
2. May take practice lessons (recommended)
3. Pass practical driving test
4. Submit conversion application

CONVERSION WITH FULL TESTS
--------------------------
All other countries: Both theory and practical test required.

This includes:
- Most Asian countries (except Japan, South Korea, Taiwan, Singapore)
- Most African countries (except Namibia, South Africa)
- Most South American countries
- China, India, Russia, etc.

GETTING A GERMAN LICENSE FROM SCRATCH
-------------------------------------

Step 1: First Aid Course (Erste Hilfe Kurs)
- 9 hours, one day
- Cost: ~€30-50
- Valid indefinitely
- Providers: Red Cross, Malteser, ASB, private schools

Step 2: Eye Test (Sehtest)
- At any optician
- Cost: ~€7
- Valid: 2 years

Step 3: Biometric Photos
- 35x45mm
- At photo shops or photo booths
- Cost: ~€10-15

Step 4: Register at Driving School (Fahrschule)
- Shop around for prices
- Can negotiate package deals
- Look for English-speaking schools in major cities

Step 5: Theory Lessons
- Minimum 14 sessions (90 minutes each)
- 12 general sessions
- 2 additional for Class B (car)
- Can be done in English (ask school)

Step 6: Theory Exam
- Multiple choice, computer-based
- Available in English, Turkish, Arabic, Russian, and more
- 30 questions, max 10 error points
- Cost: ~€25
- Book through driving school

Step 7: Practical Lessons
- Minimum required:
  - 12 normal lessons (45 min each)
  - 5 country road (Überlandfahrt)
  - 4 highway (Autobahnfahrt)
  - 3 night driving (Nachtfahrt)
- Most students need more

Step 8: Practical Exam
- Duration: ~45 minutes
- Examiner gives directions
- Must demonstrate safe driving
- Cost: ~€120
- Book through driving school

TOTAL COSTS (New License)
-------------------------
Rough estimates for Class B (car):

First Aid Course: €30-50
Eye Test: €7
Photos: €10-15
Driving School Registration: €100-300
Theory Materials: €50-100
Theory Lessons: €200-400
Practical Lessons: €1,500-3,000 (varies greatly!)
Theory Exam: €25
Practical Exam: €120
Application Fee: €45

Total: €2,000-4,500 depending on city and lessons needed

Cities like Munich/Hamburg are more expensive.
Budget €2,500-4,000 to be safe.

THEORY TEST IN ENGLISH
----------------------
Available languages:
German, English, French, Greek, Italian, Polish, Portuguese,
Romanian, Russian, Croatian, Spanish, Turkish, Arabic, and more.

How to prepare:
- Official question catalog (~1,200 questions)
- Apps: Fahrschulcard, ADAC, iTheorie
- Practice until consistently passing
- Questions are EXACTLY what's on test

PRACTICAL TEST TIPS
-------------------
Common fail reasons:
- Not checking mirrors enough
- Rolling through stop signs
- Wrong priority/right-of-way
- Speeding in 30 zones
- Forgetting to signal

Tips:
- Exaggerate mirror checks
- Full stop at stop signs
- Watch for 30 zone signs
- Stay calm, follow instructions
- Practice in exam area

PROBATIONARY PERIOD (PROBEZEIT)
-------------------------------
New drivers under 21 or first-time license holders:
- 2-year probationary period
- Zero alcohol tolerance (0.0‰)
- Violations can extend probation
- May require additional training if violations

LICENSE CLASSES
---------------
Class B: Car (up to 3.5 tons)
Class B196: Small motorcycles (125cc)
Class A: All motorcycles
Class BE: Car with trailer
Class C: Truck
Class D: Bus

Most people need Class B for regular car.
