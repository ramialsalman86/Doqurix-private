EU BLUE CARD (BLAUE KARTE EU) - GERMANY
========================================
Source: Federal Office for Migration and Refugees (BAMF), Ausländerbehörde
Last Updated: January 2026

WHAT IS THE EU BLUE CARD?
-------------------------
The EU Blue Card is a residence permit for highly qualified non-EU workers.
It allows you to live and work in Germany and provides a fast track to 
permanent residence and EU mobility.

Legal basis: § 18g Aufenthaltsgesetz (AufenthG) - Residence Act

WHO IS ELIGIBLE?
----------------
1. Non-EU/EEA citizens
2. Must have a recognized university degree:
   - German degree, OR
   - Foreign degree recognized as equivalent (check anabin.de database)
   - Or comparable qualified professional experience (minimum 3 years in IT sector)

3. Must have a job offer or employment contract in Germany
4. Job must match your qualification (relevant to your degree)

SALARY REQUIREMENTS 2025/2026
-----------------------------
Standard salary threshold: €45,300 gross per year (2025)
- This threshold is 50% of the annual contribution ceiling for pension insurance
- Adjusted annually

Shortage occupation threshold: €41,041.80 gross per year (2025)
- Lower threshold for jobs in shortage occupations:
  * IT specialists
  * Engineers
  * Natural scientists
  * Mathematicians
  * Medical doctors (except dentists)
  * Academic healthcare professionals

Note: Check current thresholds at bamf.de as they change annually

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
------------------
1. Valid passport (valid for at least 3 months beyond visa validity)
2. Biometric passport photos (35x45mm)
3. Completed application form (Antrag auf Erteilung eines Aufenthaltstitels)
4. Proof of university degree:
   - Original degree certificate
   - Transcript
   - Recognition/evaluation from anabin.de or KMK (if foreign degree)
5. Employment contract or binding job offer showing:
   - Job title and description
   - Gross annual salary
   - Start date
   - Working hours (usually full-time required)
6. Health insurance proof:
   - German statutory health insurance, OR
   - Private health insurance meeting German requirements
7. Proof of address (Meldebescheinigung if already in Germany)
8. Previous residence permits (if applicable)
9. CV/Resume

FEES
----
- First issuance: €100
- Extension: €93
- Permanent residence after Blue Card: €113

PROCESSING TIME
---------------
- If applying from within Germany: 4-12 weeks
- If applying at German embassy abroad: 2-6 weeks for visa, then Blue Card in Germany
- Fast-track (§ 81a AufenthG): Can be expedited

VALIDITY
--------
- Initially issued for 4 years
- If employment contract is shorter: Blue Card valid for contract duration + 3 months
- Renewable

PATH TO PERMANENT RESIDENCE
---------------------------
Blue Card holders can get permanent residence (Niederlassungserlaubnis) faster:

After 33 months with Blue Card + basic German (A1):
- Niederlassungserlaubnis eligible

After 21 months with Blue Card + German level B1:
- Fast-track Niederlassungserlaubnis eligible

Requirements for permanent residence:
- Contributions to pension insurance for the required period
- Adequate living space
- Basic knowledge of German legal and social system
- No criminal record

JOB CHANGE RULES
----------------
First 12 months (restricted):
- Must get approval from Ausländerbehörde before changing jobs
- Approval usually granted if new job meets Blue Card requirements

After 12 months (unrestricted):
- Can change jobs freely within Blue Card conditions
- No approval needed, but must notify Ausländerbehörde

FAMILY REUNIFICATION
--------------------
Blue Card holders have privileged family reunification:
- Spouse can join without German knowledge requirement
- Spouse gets immediate work permit
- Children under 18 can join
- Faster processing than other visa categories

EU MOBILITY
-----------
After 12 months with Blue Card:
- Can move to another EU country for Blue Card employment
- Simplified procedures
- Some restrictions apply (country-specific)

COMPARISON: BLUE CARD VS. REGULAR WORK VISA
-------------------------------------------
Blue Card advantages:
✓ Faster path to permanent residence (21-33 months vs. 60 months)
✓ Better family reunification rules
✓ EU-wide mobility rights
✓ No labor market test required
✓ More prestigious status

Regular work visa (§ 18a/b AufenthG):
- No minimum salary requirement
- More flexible job requirements
- Longer path to permanent residence
- No EU mobility rights

APPLICATION PROCESS
-------------------
If outside Germany:
1. Apply for Blue Card visa at German embassy/consulate
2. Receive visa (valid 6 months)
3. Enter Germany
4. Register address (Anmeldung)
5. Apply for Blue Card at Ausländerbehörde
6. Receive Blue Card

If already in Germany with valid residence permit:
1. Book appointment at Ausländerbehörde
2. Submit application with documents
3. Receive Blue Card (or fiction certificate while processing)

COMMON REJECTION REASONS
------------------------
1. Salary below threshold
2. Degree not recognized
3. Job not matching qualification
4. Missing documents
5. Health insurance not meeting requirements
