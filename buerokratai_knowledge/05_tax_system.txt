TAX ID, TAX CLASS, AND INCOME TAX IN GERMANY
=============================================
Source: Bundeszentralamt für Steuern (BZSt), Finanzamt
Last Updated: January 2026

STEUER-IDENTIFIKATIONSNUMMER (TAX ID)
-------------------------------------

What is it?
- 11-digit unique tax identification number
- Assigned to every person registered in Germany
- Permanent - never changes, even if you move
- Format: XX XXX XXX XXX (e.g., 12 345 678 901)

When do you get it?
- Automatically sent after your first Anmeldung (address registration)
- Arrives by mail within 2-4 weeks to your registered address
- Children born in Germany get it automatically

Why do you need it?
- Required by employer (Arbeitgeber) to calculate tax
- Required for opening bank accounts (since 2011)
- Required for tax returns
- Required for some government benefits

Lost your Tax ID?
- Request a new letter: www.bzst.de
- Online form available
- Takes about 4 weeks
- Or call BZSt: +49 228 406-1240

STEUERNUMMER (TAX NUMBER)
-------------------------
Different from Tax ID!
- Assigned by local Finanzamt (tax office)
- Format: XXX/XXX/XXXXX (varies by federal state)
- Can change if you move to different Finanzamt area
- Used on tax returns and business tax matters

TAX CLASSES (STEUERKLASSEN)
---------------------------

Germany has 6 tax classes determining monthly withholding:

TAX CLASS I
- Who: Single, divorced, widowed
- Who: Married but spouse lives abroad permanently
- Monthly withholding: Standard rate

TAX CLASS II
- Who: Single parents with child(ren) in household
- Benefit: Additional allowance (Entlastungsbetrag)
- Monthly withholding: Lower than Class I
- Must have child registered at same address
- Must apply for it (not automatic)

TAX CLASS III
- Who: Married, one spouse earns significantly more
- Combined with Class V for other spouse
- Monthly withholding: Lowest (most money monthly)
- Best when one spouse earns 60%+ of combined income

TAX CLASS IV
- Who: Married couples with similar income
- Default class for married couples
- Both spouses pay same rate
- Option: IV with factor (faktor) for optimized withholding

TAX CLASS V
- Who: Spouse of person in Class III
- Monthly withholding: Highest
- Should earn less than spouse in Class III

TAX CLASS VI
- Who: For second or additional jobs
- Monthly withholding: Highest (no allowances)
- All additional employment uses this class

MARRIED COUPLES - CHOOSING TAX CLASSES
--------------------------------------
Options for married couples:
1. III / V: Best if one earns much more (60%+ of total)
2. IV / IV: Best if both earn similar amounts
3. IV / IV with factor: Optimized based on actual income ratio

To change tax class:
- Submit "Antrag auf Steuerklassenwechsel" to Finanzamt
- Or use ELSTER online portal
- Can change once per year (exceptions for major life events)
- Marriage: Automatic change to IV/IV, can then request III/V

INCOME TAX (EINKOMMENSTEUER)
----------------------------

Progressive tax rate (2024/2025):

Income (€/year)    | Tax Rate
-------------------|----------
0 - 11,604         | 0% (tax-free allowance)
11,605 - 17,005    | 14% - 24% (progressive)
17,006 - 66,760    | 24% - 42% (progressive)
66,761 - 277,825   | 42%
Above 277,826      | 45% (Reichensteuer)

Additional:
- Solidarity surcharge (Solidaritätszuschlag): 5.5% of income tax
  - Only applies if tax exceeds ~€18,000/year
- Church tax (Kirchensteuer): 8-9% of income tax
  - Only if registered with church (Catholic, Protestant)
  - Can opt out (Kirchenaustritt)

MONTHLY TAX CALCULATION
-----------------------
Your employer calculates monthly withholding (Lohnsteuer) based on:
- Gross salary
- Tax class
- Tax ID (for electronic retrieval of data)
- Church membership
- Number of children (Kinderfreibetrag)

Salary slip (Gehaltsabrechnung) shows:
- Brutto (gross) salary
- Lohnsteuer (income tax withheld)
- Solidaritätszuschlag (solidarity surcharge)
- Kirchensteuer (church tax, if applicable)
- Sozialabgaben (social security contributions)
- Netto (net) salary

TAX RETURN (STEUERERKLÄRUNG)
----------------------------

Who MUST file:
- Self-employed
- Freelancers
- Multiple employers in one year
- Received unemployment benefits
- Tax Class III/V combination
- Income from abroad
- Received certain capital gains

Who SHOULD file (voluntary):
- Employees (often get refund ~€1,000 on average)
- Those with work-related expenses
- Those who moved for work
- Double household (doppelte Haushaltsführung)

Deadline:
- July 31 of following year (self-filing)
- End of February two years later (if using Steuerberater)
- Example: 2024 taxes due by July 31, 2025

How to file:
1. ELSTER (www.elster.de) - free government portal
2. Tax software (WISO, Taxfix, SteuerGo)
3. Steuerberater (tax advisor) - costs €100-500+

COMMON TAX DEDUCTIONS
---------------------
Employment expenses (Werbungskosten):
- Commuting (Pendlerpauschale): €0.30/km (first 20km), €0.38/km after
- Work equipment (laptop, desk, etc.)
- Professional development courses
- Work clothes (if specialized)
- Moving costs for work
- Double household

Special expenses (Sonderausgaben):
- Church tax paid
- Pension contributions
- Health/nursing insurance contributions
- Charitable donations
- Education/training costs

Extraordinary expenses (Außergewöhnliche Belastungen):
- Medical expenses above threshold
- Disability-related costs
- Care costs for family members

TAX FOR DIFFERENT SITUATIONS
----------------------------

New in Germany:
- Full tax liability starts from first day of residence
- Inform employer of Tax ID (employer can request it)
- First year may allow split tax assessment

Leaving Germany:
- Remain tax liable for German income
- May get partial year tax-free allowance
- Final tax return for departure year
- De-registration (Abmeldung) documents needed

Working remotely for foreign company:
- Complex rules apply
- May create tax liability in Germany
- Consult Steuerberater
