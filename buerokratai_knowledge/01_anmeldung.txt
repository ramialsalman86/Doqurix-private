ANMELDUNG (ADDRESS REGISTRATION) IN GERMANY
============================================
Source: Official German Government (service.berlin.de, bürgeramt.de)
Last Updated: January 2026

WHAT IS ANMELDUNG?
------------------
Anmeldung is the mandatory registration of your residence address in Germany. 
By German law (Bundesmeldegesetz - BMG), everyone who moves to Germany or changes 
their address within Germany MUST register within 14 days of moving in.

LEGAL REQUIREMENT
-----------------
- Deadline: Within 14 DAYS of moving into your new address
- Penalty for late registration: Fine up to €1,000 (in practice often €10-50)
- Legal basis: § 17 Bundesmeldegesetz (BMG)

WHO MUST REGISTER?
------------------
- All German citizens moving to a new address
- All foreign nationals staying in Germany longer than 3 months
- EU/EEA citizens planning to stay longer than 3 months
- Non-EU citizens with any type of visa or residence permit

EXEMPTIONS
----------
- Tourists staying less than 3 months (90 days)
- Business travelers on short trips
- People staying in hotels (hotel does registration)

WHERE TO REGISTER
-----------------
- Bürgeramt (Citizens' Office) / Einwohnermeldeamt
- You must register at the Bürgeramt responsible for your district
- Appointment usually required (online booking)

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
------------------
1. Valid passport or national ID card (EU citizens)
2. Anmeldeformular (registration form) - available at Bürgeramt or online
3. Wohnungsgeberbestätigung (landlord confirmation)
   - This is MANDATORY since November 2015
   - Your landlord must provide this within 2 weeks of move-in
   - Contains: landlord name, address of apartment, move-in date, your name
4. Rental contract (Mietvertrag) - recommended but not always required
5. Marriage certificate (if registering spouse)
6. Birth certificates (if registering children)

WOHNUNGSGEBERBESTÄTIGUNG (LANDLORD CONFIRMATION)
------------------------------------------------
Legal requirement since 01.11.2015 (§19 BMG)
Must contain:
- Name and address of the landlord (Wohnungsgeber)
- Address of the apartment
- Date of move-in (Einzugsdatum)
- Names of all persons moving in
- Landlord's signature

If landlord refuses to provide:
- Landlords are legally obligated to provide this
- Refusal can result in fine up to €1,000 for landlord
- Contact Bürgeramt if landlord refuses

COST
----
- Anmeldung itself: FREE (kostenlos)
- Meldebescheinigung (registration certificate): €5-15 depending on city

AFTER REGISTRATION - WHAT YOU RECEIVE
-------------------------------------
1. Anmeldebestätigung (registration confirmation) - given immediately
2. Meldebescheinigung (registration certificate) - request separately if needed
3. Your Tax ID (Steuer-ID) will be sent by mail within 2-4 weeks
   - Sent automatically to first-time registrants in Germany
   - Goes to your registered address

IMPORTANT NOTES
---------------
- Keep your Anmeldebestätigung safe - you need it for many things:
  * Bank account opening
  * Employment contracts
  * Health insurance registration
  * Residence permit applications
  * Driver's license conversion

- If you move within Germany, you must do Ummeldung (re-registration) within 14 days
- If you leave Germany permanently, you must do Abmeldung (de-registration)

COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID
------------------------
1. Missing the 14-day deadline
2. Not having Wohnungsgeberbestätigung
3. Registering at wrong Bürgeramt (must be your district)
4. Not booking appointment in advance (long wait times)

CITY-SPECIFIC INFORMATION
-------------------------
Berlin: Appointments very difficult to get - book weeks in advance
Munich: Multiple Kreisverwaltungsreferat (KVR) locations
Frankfurt: Can sometimes get same-day appointments
Hamburg: Kundenzentrum in each district
