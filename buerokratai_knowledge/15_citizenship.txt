GERMAN CITIZENSHIP AND NATURALIZATION (EINBÜRGERUNG)
====================================================
Source: Nationality Act (StAG), BAMF, BVA
Last Updated: January 2026

IMPORTANT UPDATE - NEW LAW 2024
-------------------------------
Germany updated its citizenship law in June 2024.
Key changes:
- Reduced residence requirement from 8 to 5 years
- Allows dual citizenship (multiple nationalities)
- Faster track for special integration (3 years)

WAYS TO BECOME A GERMAN CITIZEN
-------------------------------

1. By Descent (Abstammung)
   - Born to German parent
   - Automatic citizenship

2. By Birth in Germany (Geburtsrecht since 2000)
   - Born in Germany
   - At least one parent legally resident 8+ years
   - Parent has permanent residence or citizenship

3. By Naturalization (Einbürgerung)
   - Most common path for immigrants
   - Must meet requirements below

NATURALIZATION REQUIREMENTS (NEW LAW 2024)
-----------------------------------------

1. RESIDENCE: 5 years legal residence in Germany
   - Reduced from 8 years!
   - Fast track: 3 years possible (see below)
   - Must have permanent or qualifying permit

2. RESIDENCE PERMIT: Valid residence title
   - Settlement permit (Niederlassungserlaubnis) OR
   - EU Blue Card OR
   - Other qualifying permit

3. SUPPORT YOURSELF: No public benefits dependency
   - Must support yourself and family
   - Exception: Not your fault (illness, age, etc.)
   - Temporary unemployment may be okay

4. GERMAN LANGUAGE: B1 level
   - Proven by certificate OR
   - German school diploma OR
   - German university degree

5. CIVIC KNOWLEDGE: Pass citizenship test OR
   - Successfully complete Integration course OR
   - German school diploma (Hauptschulabschluss or higher)

6. NO CRIMINAL RECORD: Clean background
   - No serious convictions
   - Minor offenses may be overlooked

7. COMMITMENT TO CONSTITUTION:
   - Accept democratic values
   - Declare loyalty to Basic Law

8. RENOUNCE PREVIOUS CITIZENSHIP:
   - NOT REQUIRED ANYMORE (since 2024)!
   - Dual/multiple citizenship now allowed

FAST TRACK NATURALIZATION (3 YEARS)
-----------------------------------
Can apply after just 3 years if:
- B2 German level or higher
- Exceptional integration achievements:
  - Voluntary work
  - Professional success
  - Special civic contributions

CITIZENSHIP TEST (EINBÜRGERUNGSTEST)
------------------------------------
Test Details:
- 33 questions from pool of 310
- 30 general + 3 about your state
- 17 correct answers needed to pass (50%)
- Multiple choice
- 60 minutes
- Cost: €25

Topics:
- Living in a democracy
- History and responsibility
- People and society

Preparation:
- Practice tests: www.bpb.de/einbuergerungstest
- Free online courses available
- Question catalog is public

Where to take test:
- Adult education centers (VHS)
- BAMF-authorized providers

Exemptions from test:
- German school diploma
- Completed integration course
- University degree (German studies)
- Illness/disability

REQUIRED DOCUMENTS
------------------
Typical documents needed:
- Valid passport
- Residence permit
- Birth certificate
- Marriage certificate (if applicable)
- German language certificate (B1)
- Citizenship test certificate
- Employment contract/income proof
- Pension contribution statements
- No criminal record (requested by authority)
- Biometric photos
- Application form

All foreign documents need:
- Certified translation
- Apostille/legalization

APPLICATION PROCESS
-------------------

Step 1: Prepare documents
- Gather all required papers
- Get translations and certifications
- Take citizenship test

Step 2: Submit application
- To local Einbürgerungsbehörde
- Usually part of Ausländerbehörde
- Fee: €255 per adult, €51 per minor

Step 3: Processing
- Authority reviews application
- May request additional documents
- Background check conducted

Step 4: Decision
- If approved: Invitation to ceremony
- Receive certificate (Einbürgerungsurkunde)
- You're now German!

Timeline:
- Processing: 6-24 months (varies by location)
- Berlin/Munich: Often longer wait
- Smaller cities: Often faster

COSTS
-----
Application fee: €255 per adult
Minor child: €51
Citizenship test: €25
Language test: ~€150 (if needed)
Document translations: €50-200+
Notarizations: €20-50+

Total: Approximately €400-700

DUAL CITIZENSHIP (NEW 2024!)
----------------------------
Germany now allows dual citizenship!
- Keep your original nationality
- No need to renounce
- Children can have multiple citizenships

Before 2024:
- Had to give up other citizenship
- Exceptions for EU citizens and hardship cases

Now:
- No restrictions on multiple nationalities
- All naturalization applicants can keep original

EU CITIZENS
-----------
Always allowed to keep EU citizenship when naturalizing.
Now also applies to non-EU citizens.

CHILDREN
--------

Children born in Germany:
- To German parent: Automatic citizen
- To foreign parents (legal residents 8+ years): 
  Can have German citizenship from birth

Naturalization of children:
- Can be included in parent's application
- Reduced fee: €51
- Must also meet requirements (age-appropriate)

After parent naturalizes:
- Can apply for children separately
- Often faster process

SPECIAL PATHS
-------------

Spouses of German Citizens:
- No special shorter path (was removed)
- Same 5-year requirement
- But: Spouse's income counts toward support requirement

Former German Citizens:
- Can regain citizenship
- Simplified process
- Especially for Nazi-era victims

Late Resettlers (Spätaussiedler):
- Simplified naturalization
- Often immediate citizenship
- German heritage from Eastern Europe

LOSS OF CITIZENSHIP
-------------------
You LOSE German citizenship if:
- Voluntarily acquire another citizenship (only pre-2024 cases)
- Join foreign military without permission
- Adopt another nationality with intent to lose German

You DON'T lose citizenship:
- Living abroad permanently
- Not using German passport
- Marrying non-German

BENEFITS OF GERMAN CITIZENSHIP
------------------------------
✓ EU citizenship (live/work anywhere in EU)
✓ German passport (strong worldwide)
✓ Vote in all German elections
✓ No visa worries/residence permit renewal
✓ Full social welfare access
✓ Cannot be deported
✓ Pass citizenship to children

COMMON REJECTION REASONS
------------------------
✗ Gaps in residence history
✗ Insufficient income/public benefits
✗ Criminal convictions
✗ Failed language test
✗ Failed citizenship test
✗ Insufficient integration evidence
✗ Incomplete documentation
